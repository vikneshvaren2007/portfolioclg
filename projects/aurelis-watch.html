AURELIS

A premium full-stack watch e-commerce platform built with a luxury-focused interface, customer accounts, shopping workflows, checkout, order management and an administrative dashboard.

Project Overview

AURELIS-WATCH is a bespoke haute horlogerie digital showcase engineered for luxury mechanical chronograph timepieces. Designed with deep obsidian surfaces, satin gold metallic finishes, and razor-sharp typographic hierarchy, the site bridges fine watchmaking with high-performance web engineering.

Featuring the signature Dragon Chronograph Collection, the experience provides collectors with macro-resolution dial inspection, caliber movement telemetry, water resistance certification breakdowns, and direct VIP concierge inquiry workflows.

Problem & Strategic Purpose

High-end horological manufactures face the challenge of translating physical mechanical luxury into the digital realm without feeling like a generic e-commerce template. Collectors expect prestige, micro-mechanical precision, and immediate concierge access.

AURELIS-WATCH solves this by:

Macro Horology Inspection

Razor-sharp visual showcase highlighting the sculpted dragon dial, multi-layer chronograph sub-dials, and fluted bezel.

Caliber Telemetry

Interactive mechanical movement breakdown detailing beat frequency, power reserve, and jewels.

Concierge VIP Inquiries

Frictionless WhatsApp and direct concierge reservations pre-configured with the client's selected timepiece.

Certificate & Provenance

Authentication verification workflow highlighting serial registration and chronometer precision standards.

Main Features & Engineering Details

  • High-Resolution Dial Visualizer: Precision framing of the 18K gold-plated dragon relief, chronograph subdials, and luminous hands.
  • Tactile Caliber Specifications: Clean tabular breakdown of case diameter (48mm), sapphire crystal coating, 200M water resistance, and triple-link solid bracelet.
  • Bespoke Obsidian & Gold Design System: Dark aesthetic with subtle metallic gold gradients, zero eye strain, and ultra-high contrast for horological photography.
  • Instant Concierge WhatsApp Workflow: Automated direct WhatsApp routing passing the exact watch model and collector inquiry parameters.
