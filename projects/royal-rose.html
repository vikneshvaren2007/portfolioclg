ROYAL ROSE

A sensory digital brand experience celebrating Damascus rose petal infusions, organic farm dairy selections, and interactive essence customizer technology.

Project Overview

ROYAL ROSE is a luxury landing page and sensory e-commerce experience crafted for an artisanal rose milk brand. Designed with delicate blush tones, deep obsidian accents, and fine gold filigree, the site delivers a high-end editorial atmosphere tailored for premium gastronomy.

Beyond its aesthetic beauty, the platform incorporates an interactive client-side Custom Blend Configurator that computes ingredient ratios, botanical essence percentages, and dynamic pricing updates in real-time.

Problem & Strategic Purpose

Beverage and culinary websites often suffer from static product pages that fail to evoke the tactile senses of aroma, origin, and craftsmanship.

ROYAL ROSE was designed to:

Sensory Storytelling

Combine fluid typography and macro imagery to communicate the purity of distilled Damascus petals.

Interactive Formulation

Allow customers to customize petal essence ratio (30% to 95%) with live pricing recalculated instantly.

Botanical Transparency

Highlight pure ingredients — organic cane sugar, wild honey, Whole Farm milk, silky almond milk, and oat milk.

Mobile-First Fluidity

Zero-lag responsive touch interactions with butter-smooth slider feedback across all viewport sizes.

Main Features & Technical Capabilities

  • Real-Time Blend Configurator: Custom range slider adjusting Damask essence concentration with automatic recipe description generation.
  • Dynamic Price Matrix: Real-time price updates (e.g. ₹120 base, +₹20 pure honey, +₹30 almond milk base) handled without page reloads.
  • Art-Directed Visual Layout: High-resolution product showcase featuring custom photography, subtle radial glows, and refined gold dividers.
  • Optimized Asset Loading: Lightweight vanilla CSS transitions and lazy-loaded assets ensuring instant sub-second load times.
